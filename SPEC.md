requirements and design entry point. See its [register](docs/design/REQUIREMENTS.md),
[decision history](docs/design/DECISIONS.md) and [verification gates](docs/design/VERIFICATION.md).

by the maintainer on 2026-09-27. Historical documents remain available for
archaeology; see the [documentation map](docs/CANONICAL_DOCS.md).
